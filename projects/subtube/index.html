SubTube

A YouTube subscription manager — a calmer way to follow the channels you've actually chosen, outside the algorithm's feed.

Role
Design, product & build

Status
In development
View on GitHub

Why I built it

YouTube's home feed is built to maximise watch time, not to show you what the channels you subscribe to have published. Subscriptions pile up over the years, and the subscription feed itself gives you no way to organise, triage, or prune them. SubTube starts from the opposite premise: your subscriptions are a reading list you curate, not an input to a recommender.

What it does

  • Subscription inventory — pull your full subscription list via the YouTube API and see it in one place.
  • Organise & prune — group channels, spot the ones you never watch, and unsubscribe deliberately.
  • Intentional watching — browse new uploads channel-by-channel instead of through the algorithmic feed.

How it's built

Python against the YouTube Data API, developed with an AI-assisted workflow. The interesting product constraint is the API's quota system — the design batches and caches channel data so a daily check-in costs a fraction of the quota a naive implementation would burn.

Status & what's next

Working tool, evolving in the open. Next up: a simple web UI on top of the current core.
