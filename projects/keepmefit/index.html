KeepMeFit

An iOS app for logging meals, tracking the day's nutrient breakdown, and building a personal meal catalog from a food library.

Role
Design, product & build
Stack
Swift · SwiftUI · iOS
Status
In development
View on GitHub

Why I built it

Most nutrition apps optimise for massive food databases and calorie streaks, but day-to-day eating is repetitive: the same home-cooked meals, in rotation. I wanted a tracker built around meals I actually eat — log a day in a few taps, see the nutrient picture at a glance, and stop doing data entry for the same dal-and-rice every week.

What it does

  • Daily log — record meals per day and see the day's nutrient breakdown.
  • Meal catalog — compose reusable meals from a food library once, log them forever.
  • Suggestions — discover meal ideas that fit the day's remaining budget.
App screenshots coming soon

How it's built

Native Swift and SwiftUI, developed with an AI-assisted workflow — agentic coding tools for scaffolding and iteration, with product and interface decisions made by hand. The data model separates the food library, the meal catalog, and the daily log, so logging stays a two-tap action rather than a search-and-measure chore.

Status & what's next

In active development. Next up: refining the suggestion logic and preparing a TestFlight build.
